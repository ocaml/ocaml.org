<p>Chloe Casale was planning to be an auto mechanic before taking an intro CS class on a bet. After a stint as a Forward Deployed Engineer at Palantir, Chloe came to Jane Street with no background in OCaml or finance. Now she leads a team of “desk devs” building tools for client-facing traders. In this episode, Ron and Chloe discuss how a surprising amount of Jane Street’s client business is conducted via chat, and the promise and perils of using LLMs to automate that trading. They dive deep on hiring and interviewing: does Jane Street’s process select for less “spiky” kinds of people than Palantir’s? Along the way, they marvel at Webs, an internal spreadsheet-like framework for composing computations and building UIs (which, you can see, is sort of hard to explain); compare building software for internal versus external customers; and recount the tale of Chloe’s epic 1,000km horse race across the Mongolian steppe.</p><p>You can find the transcript for this episode on our <a href="https://signalsandthreads.com/trading-at-the-speed-of-chat/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">website</a>.</p><p>Some links to topics that came up in the discussion:</p><ul><li><a href="https://equestrianists.com/mongol-derby/" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Mongol Derby</a></li><li><a href="https://github.com/janestreet/bonsai/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bonsai</a></li><li><a href="https://www.infoq.com/presentations/slang/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Slang</a></li><li><a href="https://www.janestreet.com/preparing-for-a-software-engineering-interview/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Preparing for a Software Engineering interview at Jane Street</a></li><li><a href="https://blog.palantir.com/a-day-in-the-life-of-a-palantir-forward-deployed-software-engineer-45ef2de257b1" rel="noopener noreferrer">A Day in the Life of a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer</a></li></ul>
