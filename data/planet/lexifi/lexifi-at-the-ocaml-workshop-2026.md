<p>The <a href="https://ocaml.org/ocaml-workshop-2026">2026 edition of the OCaml Workshop</a>
is taking place on August 24 in Paris and LexiFi is taking part in it.</p>
<h3>Talk: Runtime Types at LexiFi</h3>
<p>The 20-minute talk will be a presentation of LexiFi’s system of <em>runtime types</em>,
the type reflection machinery of our compiler fork that underpins a large part
of our software stack.</p>
